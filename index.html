Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

This Application operates independently. We are committed to protecting user privacy during Roblox OAuth 2.0 authentication.

1. Information We Collect

Via the openid profile scope, we only access your unique Roblox User ID, Username, Display Name, Profile URL, and Avatar Thumbnail.

2. How We Use Your Information

Data is used strictly to customize your local dashboard interface and verify your account ownership. We do not engage in user profiling or cross-platform tracking.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your individual user data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

4. Data Deletion

You can revoke access at any time through your Roblox account settings. To request permanent removal of your cached user ID from our system, contact us via our support channels.
